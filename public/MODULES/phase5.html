Lesson 5.2

AI Landing Page Creation

AI can help generate website copy, headlines, calls-to-action, and page layouts in minutes. Instead of writing everything manually, you can use AI tools to create professional landing pages faster.

What is a Landing Page?

A landing page is a standalone web page designed to guide visitors toward a specific action such as signing up, booking a service, or purchasing a product.

Example: Create a landing page for a local coffee shop with a modern design, customer testimonials, menu highlights, and a strong call-to-action.

Essential Landing Page Sections

  • Hero Section
  • Benefits Section
  • Features or Services
  • Testimonials
  • Call-To-Action
  • Contact Information

Benefits of AI Website Creation

  • Faster website development
  • Professional copywriting assistance
  • Improved design ideas
  • Beginner-friendly workflow
  • Reduced content creation time

Lesson 5.3

AI Presentation Design Principles

AI tools can generate presentation slides in seconds, but effective presentations still require clear structure, strong messaging, and visual consistency.

What Makes a Great Presentation?

Great presentations communicate ideas clearly, keep the audience engaged, and focus on key information without overwhelming viewers with too much text.

Example: Create a 10-slide presentation about AI Productivity Tools for small business owners. Include a cover slide, benefits, real-world examples, and a call-to-action.

Presentation Best Practices

  • Use clear slide titles
  • Keep text concise
  • Use high-quality visuals
  • Maintain consistent branding
  • Highlight key takeaways

Common AI Presentation Use Cases

  • Business Presentations
  • Sales Pitches
  • Training Materials
  • Educational Content
  • Marketing Reports

Your Mission

  • Business Name: Define the business or brand.
